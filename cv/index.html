Research Interests

  • Degradation of Polymers and Composites
    • Transport phenomena in crosslinked resins (e.g., acid, ions).
    • Oxidative degradation kinetics in linear polymers considering the effect of mechanical stress.
  • Recycling of Polymers and Composites
    • Dissolution mechanisms and kinetics of polymers in solution.
    • Approaches to efficient separation/recovery of end-of-life multi-phase materials.
  • Development of Sustainable Polymers and Composites
    • Utilization of biomass (e.g., lignin, cellulose, small molecules) in high-performance composites.
    • Structural control through noncovalent interactions between phases and processing conditions.
  • Development of Functional Polymers and Composites
    • Novel thermally conductive thermoplastic epoxy-based polymers with highly crystalline structure.
    • Hybrid and multi-layered nanocomposites (i.e., organic/inorganic) for improved dielectric or thermal properties.
